Believer, Please Try Again With 11 Seeds 🌱💜
A gentle reminder that a new attempt can begin with faith, effort, and love
Dear Believer,
My child, you have tried your best. Your seed was not successfully sown, but this does not mean that your good faith has disappeared. Sometimes faith can feel smaller when an attempt does not go as hoped.

YOUR SOULMATE IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN ANY MONEY. 

No amount in your bank account will comfort you if they are lost. "Money" comes and goes. But your soulmate?

 They are eternal. What is a few dollars compared to a lifetime with God's chosen one for you?

Do not let one difficult moment become the end of your intention. If you still feel this is meaningful to your heart, you can choose to try again with renewed effort, patience, prayer, and love.
Your faith can begin again 💗
A failed or incomplete attempt does not have to define what comes next. Let this be a moment to pause, gather your heart, and return with sincerity.
Try again not from fear or pressure, but from good faith, effort, prayer, hope, and love. Sowing can be a personal symbol of choosing to begin again.
For your new attempt
Sow 11 Seeds 🌱
If it feels right to your heart, consider sowing 11 seeds again as a renewed expression of your intention.
Let each seed represent renewed faith, sincere effort, patience, hope, kindness, and love as you move forward.
Why Sow 11 Seeds? 🌿
In this Seed Garden message, 11 seeds are a symbolic way to give your renewed intention a clear meaning. Each seed can carry one prayer, one virtue, and one hope into your next step.
1 — Faith · trusting God even when the path ahead is not yet clear.

2 — Protection · praying for safety, guidance, and care over you and the people you love.

3 — Peace · asking for calm in your heart, thoughts, choices, and relationships.

4 — Healing · releasing old pain and making space for restoration and gentleness.

5 — Hope · believing that goodness, renewal, and brighter days can still grow.

6 — Patience · allowing life, prayer, and love to unfold without forcing the outcome.

7 — Courage · finding strength to keep moving forward with sincerity and grace.

8 — Trust · letting go of fear and holding onto faith without demanding certainty.

9 — Joy · making room for gratitude, happiness, and appreciation for what is good today.

10 — Unity · praying for understanding, mutual care, and loving connection.

11 — Love · choosing kindness, compassion, loyalty, and a sincere heart.
Let the next 11 seeds represent a deliberate choice to begin again with faith, prayer, effort, hope, and love.
